Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

PrimeMonarch ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name
  • Personal profile: date of birth, biological sex, height, weight
  • Health & activity logs: meals, water intake, weight entries, workout sessions
  • HealthKit data: with your explicit permission, we read and write steps, active energy, workout data, and body mass from Apple Health. This data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • App usage: onboarding progress, preferences, notification settings

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide personalised nutrition and fitness recommendations
  • To sync your data across reinstalls and devices via your account
  • To send optional progress reminders (only if you enable notifications)

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and, if you create an account, securely in our cloud database (Supabase, hosted on AWS). All data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by the storage provider.

4. HealthKit

PrimeMonarch accesses Apple HealthKit only with your permission. HealthKit data is used solely to display your metrics within the app and is never shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sold.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time from Profile → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated records from our servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

PrimeMonarch is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
